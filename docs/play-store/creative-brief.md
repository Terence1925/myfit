# Creative brief prompt: MyFit Play Store banner and 8 phone screenshots

Paste everything under the line into a designer brief, or into an AI design or image tool.

---

You are a senior **App Store Creative Designer and ASO specialist** with 10+ years of experience making store graphics for health and fitness apps that convert well. Create a **Google Play feature graphic (banner)** and **8 phone screenshots** for the app below. They must look high end, minimalist and premium (think Apple Fitness, Whoop, Oura, Strava), never childish, cluttered or generic.

## The app
- **Name:** MyFit (store title still being finalised, so use only "MyFit" on the artwork)
- **What it is:** an all-in-one workout and calorie tracker. A personalised 12-week workout plan plus a food log with a barcode scanner and a cookbook of 93 healthy recipes with photos.
- **Audience:** adults 18 to 45 in the Philippines and similar markets who want a clear plan: beginners and intermediate gym-goers, home workouts, people watching calories. Language: English. Tone: confident, simple, motivating, not pushy.
- **Real features only (never invent any):**
  1. 12-week plan built around the person's goal, equipment (full gym, dumbbells, bodyweight, bands, yoga, Pilates) and recovery.
  2. Weekly targets: the weight to aim for, with coaching that adapts to the last session.
  3. Set logging with a rest timer that alerts even in the background.
  4. Food log with barcode scanning and search (with the weight of each serving in grams).
  5. Cookbook with photos, steps, calories and protein for every recipe.
  6. Daily calorie, protein, carb and fat target from the person's body, sleep and stress.
  7. Progress charts and personal-record alerts.
  8. Recovery score that adjusts the sets.
  Free food log and cookbook. Pro (7-day free trial) unlocks logging, progress and the full plan.

## Brand
- **Primary green:** #66C23D. Headline accent gradient: #86DC58 to #4CAB29.
- **Dark base:** #0D0E12 to #14161C. Body text grey: #A7ADBA. White for headlines.
- **Look:** dark charcoal canvas, a soft green glow behind the phone, real UI inside a modern thin-bezel phone frame, generous empty space, one idea per image. Clean sans-serif (Segoe UI Variable / Inter style), bold headlines with tight spacing, a small spaced-capitals label above each headline.

## Banner (feature graphic) - 1024 x 500 px, JPG or 24-bit PNG, no transparency
- Message: **"Train smarter. Eat on target."** with the small line **"Workout plan + calorie tracker"**.
- Logo (green square, white flexing arm) and the word MyFit top left.
- Two overlapping phones on the right showing the Home screen and the Food log, slightly tilted, bleeding off the bottom edge.
- Keep all text and the logo inside the central safe area (at least 60 px from every edge). Readable at thumbnail size.

## Rules (Google Play policy and good practice)
- Show only screens the app really has. Use a clean demo account (name "Alex", realistic sample data). No real people's data.
- Do **not** use: ratings or star icons, download counts, "No. 1", "Best", "Free" as a headline, prices, discount or promo wording, competitor names, or medical claims.
- Do not cover important parts of the UI with badges. Keep text large enough to read at thumbnail size on a phone (headline at least 90 px on a 1080 px canvas).
- Keep a consistent system across all 8 so they read as one set when scrolling. The first 3 matter most: make 1 to 3 the strongest.
